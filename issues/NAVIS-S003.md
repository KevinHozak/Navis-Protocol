## Outcome

Trial three source-linked reusable knowledge notes.

Parent: [NAVIS-E001](NAVIS-E001.md). Depends on [NAVIS-S001](NAVIS-S001.md).

## Target areas

Selected source sets, existing Vault/Library capabilities and manual evaluation.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] After the actionable-brief trial decision is recorded, produce three notes with question, findings, source links, disagreements/unknowns and one practical application.
- [ ] Trace every material factual claim to evidence; separate source statements from interpretation; record zero invented quotes, references or findings.
- [ ] Each note needs at most one correction round; test a later relevant question per note and answer at least two of three without reopening original research.
- [ ] Record later reuse as pending until actually tested, measure correction effort and record a sanitized keep/revise/drop decision.

## Evaluation, privacy and scope

Follow the parent epic's evaluation record, privacy requirements and trial gates. This issue records planned work only. Conditional implementation requires separately selected work after passing evidence; no trial results are claimed.
