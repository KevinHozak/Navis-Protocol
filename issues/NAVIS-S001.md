## Outcome

Trial actionable briefs on five rough notes.

Parent: [NAVIS-E001](NAVIS-E001.md).

## Target areas

Journal composer and manual evaluation; no application changes.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Retain five originals and produce Goal, Known facts, Uncertainties and One suggested next action; include straightforward, ambiguous and competing-concern samples.
- [ ] At least four of five briefs are usable after at most one correction; record all corrections and zero invented facts, dates or commitments, including corrected inventions.
- [ ] Separate explicit intentions from suggestions; do not create tasks, deadlines, checked items or execute actions.
- [ ] Complete the evaluation record for each sample and publish a sanitized keep/revise/drop decision with supporting evidence.
