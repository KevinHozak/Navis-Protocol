## Outcome

Measure and simplify three capture-to-decision journeys.

Parent: [NAVIS-E001](NAVIS-E001.md). Depends on [NAVIS-S005](NAVIS-S005.md).

## Target areas

Rough note to saved brief; selected sources to saved knowledge note; Holocron to chosen adjustment.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] After earlier trial decisions, review the three journeys using available workflows or preserved trial outputs; do not require a failed conditional feature to be implemented.
- [ ] Record baseline and candidate steps, completion time, correction effort and points of confusion; preserve originals and requirements.
- [ ] Improve completion time or interaction count in at least two of three journeys without increasing factual errors or correction effort.
- [ ] Retain original/source access, editing and explicit save controls; user completes each candidate journey without additional explanation.
- [ ] Record exact demonstrated changes and a sanitized keep/revise/drop decision rather than proposing a general redesign.

## Evaluation, privacy and scope

Follow the parent epic's evaluation record, privacy requirements and trial gates. This issue records planned work only. Conditional implementation requires separately selected work after passing evidence; no trial results are claimed.
